From domain phonetics to an uncropped production archive, each step executes deterministic design math without hallucinations or misaligned typography.

    Domain Semantic Decomposition

Technical Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything regarding vector assets, commercial licensing, and marketplace compatibility.

Why Are Brand Marks Cut Off on Atom.com?

Atom.com uses Cropper.js with an autoCropArea coefficient of 0.8. If a logo image occupies more than 80% of canvas dimensions, clicking "Crop & Apply" automatically discards 10% on every margin. LogoStudio.si strictly renders lockups inside the calibrated safe zone.

Do Generated Logos Include Full Vector SVG Files?

Yes. Every export package includes standalone, standards-compliant SVG files with text paths and geometric vector emblems that scale infinitely without pixelation or blur.

Can I Use LogoStudio.si for Local Slovenian Domains (.si)?

LogoStudio.si is specifically optimized for Slovenian vocabulary and national ccTLD branding, with intelligent handling of compound words and regional character typography.

What Commercial Rights Are Transferred Upon Download?

You receive 100% unrestricted worldwide commercial ownership. You can trademark your mark, integrate it into commercial software, or sell it with your domain name.
